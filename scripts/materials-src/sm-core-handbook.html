What SafeMan Core is

SafeMan Core (SM-CORE) is the flagship programme of SafeMan by Oryx, the corporate safety-training suite of Oryx Institute. It is a SAMTRAC-equivalent, five-day-equivalent management-systems ticket built around three international standards: ISO 45001:2018 for occupational health and safety management, ISO 14001:2015 for environmental control, and ISO 31000 for risk management. A cohort that completes Core can read, run and audit a safety management system rather than only comply with one.

The programme carries 40 notional hours across four modules, closing on a timed examination sat in the student portal. Learners who pass are issued a numbered certificate and a PVC pass, both sealed with a QR code that anyone — an employer, an auditor, a client — can verify in seconds without an account or a database lookup.

Core is written for HSE managers, safety officers and supervisors: the people who own the system day to day. It is the five-day spine of the thirteen-ticket SafeMan catalogue; the other twelve tickets are one-to-two-day specialist programmes that attach to it.

BENCHMARK DISCLOSURE SafeMan by Oryx is an inter-organizational industry safety benchmark aligned with ISO 45001:2018, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 31000, and pre-referenced to NTA Unit Standards 846–858 and Namibian GN 156 of 1997 (non-NQA statutory credit). It is not an NQA-accredited qualification. SAMTRAC is a trademark of NOSA (Pty) Ltd; "SAMTRAC-style" and "SAMTRAC-equivalent" are comparative descriptors. For the government pre-certified pathway, enrol with the Oryx Safety Academy NQF track.

The four modules

Four modules carry a cohort from the ISO 45001 clause map to an audited environmental register. Each module pairs the standard's language with the artefact it produces — a legal register, a BowTie diagram, a permit workflow, an aspects-and-impacts register — so the system is built on paper while it is taught.

The OHS management system architecture

MODULE C1

Plan-Do-Check-Act applied to ISO 45001:2018: context of the organisation, worker participation, documented information, and the legal register as the system's foundation.

  • ISO 45001 clause map 4–10
  • Legal & other requirements register
  • Worker consultation obligations
  • Documented information control

ISO 31000 risk & BowTie barrier thinking

MODULE C2

Risk assessment methodology from context to treatment. BowTie diagrams make threats, top events, consequences and preventive/recovery barriers explicit and auditable.

  • Risk appetite & criteria
  • BowTie: threat — top event — consequence
  • Preventive vs recovery barriers
  • Barrier health monitoring

Contractor & e-PTW governance

MODULE C3

Managing contractor safety from pre-qualification to demobilisation: safety file review, mutual responsibility matrices, and electronic permit-to-work workflows.

  • Pre-qualification criteria
  • Safety file review checklist
  • e-PTW workflow & authorities
  • Handover & close-out

ISO 14001 environmental control & auditing

MODULE C4

Environmental aspects/impacts registers, waste hierarchy, emergency preparedness for spills, and internal audit programmes aligned to ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 clause 9.

  • Aspects & impacts register
  • Waste hierarchy 4Rs
  • Spill response preparedness
  • Internal audit & MOC

The timed examination

The examination is sat in the SafeMan student portal, under a live countdown, and is drawn fresh from the question bank on every attempt. Eight questions in ten minutes; the pass mark is seven of eight. Questions are drawn from the four modules' own territory — the clause map, the BowTie, the permit workflow and the environmental register — so a pass means the material was absorbed, not memorised.

The credential

A pass issues two documents on the spot: a numbered A4 certificate and a wallet-sized PVC pass, both signed by Tangi Iigonda, Principal & Founder of Oryx Institute. SafeMan serials run in the SM-2026-NNNN series and record the learner's name, the course code and the issue date.

Every credential carries a QR code that encodes its payload and an HMAC-SHA256 signature. Scanning it opens the public verification page, which recomputes the signature and states plainly whether the document was issued by Oryx Institute — no account, no database, arithmetic-level trust. Employers and auditors can verify a SafeMan pass as easily as reading it.

Delivery and enrollment

Enrollment requests go to the registrar at Oryx Institute by email. The registrar replies with cohort dates, the invoice and a portal access code for each learner — no accounts are created and no passwords are remembered. The three registration modes below cover a single professional booking a seat and a company putting a whole cohort through the ticket.

Individual RegistrationOne learner, one seat, a scheduled public intake.
Corporate Cohort (in-house)The ticket runs at your site, for your team, on your calendar.
Corporate Cohort (public class)Your team joins a scheduled public intake.

Access codes follow the cohort format issued by the registrar (for example ORYX-2026). Learners sign into the student portal with their name and code, study the module material, sit the timed examination and download their certificate and PVC pass from the same room.

The Oryx dual-track

Organisations that want the government pre-certified pathway enrol with the Academy; organisations that want a fast, verified corporate benchmark run SafeMan tickets. Many run both.
